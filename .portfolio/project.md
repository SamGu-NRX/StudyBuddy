StudyBuddy was built for first-generation and low-income students who don’t have a tutor on call. It reads a course syllabus and uses GPT-4o to write multiple-choice questions and flashcards grounded in it, then tracks your answers to show which concepts you’re still missing. I led the five-person team at Boost Hacks II, where it won Best Education Hack and placed in the top five of 1,272 entries.
